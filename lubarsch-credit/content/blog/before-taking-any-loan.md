## Ask these questions
- How much will I receive **today**, after all fees?
- How much will I pay each day or week, and for how long?
- What is the **total** I will pay back?
- What happens if I miss a payment?

## Warning signs
- The lender asks for your **BVN PIN, ATM PIN, OTP or bank password** — never share these
- An app asks to read your **phone contacts, call logs or photos**
- You are asked to pay money into a **personal account**
- Pressure to sign today, or fees that are only explained "later"
