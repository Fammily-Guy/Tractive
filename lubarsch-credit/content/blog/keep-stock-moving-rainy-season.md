## 1. Protect your goods first
- Tarpaulin, raised pallets, sealed containers for garri, beans, rice
- Check for leaks in your stall roof before the heavy rains

## 2. Bring the market to your customers
- Take orders on WhatsApp and deliver to regular customers
- Share a daily "what I have today" status update

## 3. Stock what sells in the rain
- Pepper soup ingredients, umbrellas and nylon, quick foods
- Buy smaller quantities of goods that spoil fast

## 4. Work with your neighbours
- Share transport costs for buying stock
- Agree with nearby sellers to watch each other's stalls

## 5. Plan your repayments
- Know which days are usually slow and put aside a little on busy days
- Call your Lubarsch officer early if you see a slow week coming
