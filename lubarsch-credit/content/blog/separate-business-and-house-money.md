## Why it matters
- You can't see your real profit when everything is in one purse
- Stock money gets spent on house needs, and the shop shrinks

## Simple steps
1. Use two purses, two envelopes, or two bank accounts
2. Pay yourself a fixed "salary" from the business each week
3. Write down what you buy and what you sell — even in a small notebook
4. Keep your loan repayment money separate too
