## 1. Who we are
Lubarsch Credit ("we", "us") is the data controller for personal data collected through this website and by our officers. Contact our Data Protection Officer at **[privacy@lubarschcredit.com]** or **[office address]**.

## 2. What we collect
- **About you:** name, phone and WhatsApp numbers, gender, date of birth, home address, local government area, preferred language
- **About your business:** type of business, market, stall location, years in business, estimated daily sales range
- **Identity:** ID type and number, Bank Verification Number (BVN), optional photo of your ID or passport photo
- **Your guarantor:** name, phone number and relationship to you (please tell your guarantor you are sharing this)
- **Technical:** basic visit statistics, only if you accept analytics in the cookie banner

**We never request access to your phone contacts, call logs, SMS or photo gallery.**

## 3. Why we use it (lawful basis)
- To assess and manage your loan application (contract, and steps before a contract)
- To verify your identity and prevent fraud (legal obligation and legitimate interest)
- To contact you about your application — only if you consent
- To meet legal and regulatory obligations

We never sell your data. We never contact anyone in your phone.
