We know some lenders shame people and harass their families. **We don't, and we never will.** Here is our promise to you, in plain words.

## What we promise
- **No shaming.** We will never shout at you, embarrass you in the market, or post about you anywhere — not on WhatsApp, Facebook or anywhere else.
- **No contacting people in your phone.** We never access your phone contacts, call logs or photos. We will never message your friends, family or customers.
- **We only speak to you and your guarantor** — the person you chose — and only about your loan.
- **Only identified collectors.** Money is collected only by Lubarsch officers who show their official ID card, or through approved payment channels. You always get a receipt.
- **Reasonable hours.** We contact you only between **[8am and 6pm]**, Monday to Saturday.
- **No threats.** We will never threaten you, use force, or take your goods without a legal process.

## If you are struggling
Call or WhatsApp your officer as early as you can. We will listen and try to agree a plan that works — for example, adjusting your payment days.
